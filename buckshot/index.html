How to play

The dealer loads a known number of live and blank shells, then shuffles them into the shotgun. On your turn, use any items you like, then shoot the dealer or yourself. Shooting yourself with a blank keeps your turn. Whoever runs out of charges loses the round.

Keys: D shoot dealer, S shoot self, 1-8 use item, Enter continue. Hover an item to see what it does.

On touch screens: tap an item once to see what it does, tap again to use it. Hold your phone upright for the tall layout.
