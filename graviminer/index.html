    You are a pilot controlling a spaceship in deep space. Your goal is to collect as many "gravitanium" crystals as possible in 60 seconds. They are purplish crystals scattered around, and you will recognize them when you see them. They help make space travel possible by bending gravity.

    The mouse pointer is the centre of gravity pulling the spaceship. Hold SPACEBAR to increase the pull. Head to "Tutorial", move the pointer around and press SPACEBAR at times to get the feel for it.

    You are paid in "gravicoins": 1 per crystal on levels 2–4, 2 per crystal on levels 5–7, and 3 per crystal on level 8. The tutorial pays nothing. Spend coins to post to the Hall of Fame on the left, and check the leaderboard on the right to see how other pilots are doing.
